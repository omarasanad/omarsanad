# Medical Reel — Colonoscopy (vertical 1080×1920)

A vertical Reels/TikTok/Shorts edit of a 31 s handheld recording from an endoscopy room. The doctor speaks while the camera films the colonoscopy monitor. The edit is code: an HTML page paints every frame for a time `t`, headless Chromium screenshots each frame, and FFmpeg encodes the result. The whole edit rebuilds with one command.

## ⚠️ Spoken-word captions still need the transcript

No speech-recognition model could be downloaded. The session's network policy blocked huggingface.co, the OpenAI model CDN and the GitHub/Vosk model mirrors. The doctor's Egyptian-Arabic speech therefore **has not been transcribed**. The captions are not invented; the current render has **no spoken-word captions**. The caption system is built and tested (RTL shaping, phrase pop-in, word-by-word highlight, accent colours) and only needs the real text:

1. Put the doctor's exact words in `project/data/transcript.txt`: one short phrase per line, with medical terms marked `*like this*` (teal) or `^like this^` (orange). An optional `[start-end]` prefix pins a line's timing. Lines without timing are spread automatically over the detected speech regions.
   *Or*, on a machine that can reach huggingface.co, run `python3 scripts/transcribe.py source/source.mp4` (faster-whisper large-v3, word timestamps). Review the draft word by word against the audio, then run `python3 scripts/captions.py --asr data/asr_words.json` or copy the corrected text into `transcript.txt`.
2. Run `./build.sh` (see below). This regenerates `output/captions.srt` and the MP4 with burned-in captions.

## What was done to the footage

**Analysis.** Source: 400×368 (near-square), 30 fps, 31.13 s, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo (WhatsApp-compressed). It is one continuous handheld take with no scene cuts. The doctor is seen mostly from behind or in profile, pointing at a Pentax endoscopy monitor. The recording ends mid-phrase at its last frame. Pauses are all short (0.3–0.5 s), so **no cuts were made**: removing them would make the speech sound unnatural, and the clip has no dead air at the start.

**Patient privacy.** The monitor's info sidebar shows the patient's name, the hospital, the physician and the date. That region is **tracked and blurred** in every frame. An ORB-feature homography tracks the monitor plane (`scripts/track_monitor.py`), and hand-placed keyframes cover the camera push-in at 21.9–26.5 s (`scripts/anonymize.py`). Every frame was checked on contact sheets. On-screen titles were derived only from information visible on screen ("colon" exam field, EC-38 colonoscope). No diagnosis or finding is labelled.

**Audio.**
1. Spectral-gate noise reduction, using a noise profile taken from the natural pauses (`noisereduce`, 78 % reduction, not 100 %, to avoid a metallic voice).
2. Narrow notches on a periodic **~700 Hz monitor beep** and its harmonics. It repeats every 0.68 s, consistent with a pulse-oximeter tone.
3. Gentle EQ: high-pass 85 Hz, −2.5 dB at 220 Hz (mud), +2.5 dB at 3.2 kHz (clarity), −1.5 dB at 6.5 kHz.
4. 3:1 compression, a limiter, then loudness normalization.

Result: the speech-to-room-noise gap went from about 11.5 dB to about 20 dB. Breaths and pauses are kept.

**Music.** An original ambient underscore, **synthesized from scratch in code** (`scripts/music.py`: soft pad, gentle arpeggio, sub, synthetic reverb; D major, 84 BPM; no samples, no vocals, no drums). It is original output generated for this project, so no third-party licence is involved. It is low-passed at 3.8 kHz to stay out of the voice's presence band and side-chain ducked under the voice (median 22.8 dB, minimum 15.8 dB below the speech). It fades in and out and lifts slightly for the outro.

**Sound effects.** Subtle whooshes, ticks and chimes, also synthesized (`scripts/sfx.py`), placed on the transitions in `data/timeline.json`.

**Motion graphics** (`index.html`; deep navy / medical teal / white, cyan and orange accents, Cairo Arabic typeface):
- 0–0.6 s: panel reveal, logo spring-in, title "منظار القولون · COLONOSCOPY · من داخل غرفة المناظير".
- 2.9 s, 12.9 s, 19.8 s: controlled punch-ins (≤1.15×) on the tracked monitor, timed to the doctor pointing at the screen.
- 7.9–12.6 s: split screen. The video crops to the monitor while an anatomical colon card plays below it: a schematic of the large intestine with a scope marker travelling the colonoscopy route rectum → sigmoid → descending → transverse → ascending → cecum, with matching segment chips.
- 15.9–19.6 s: a magnifier callout enlarges the endoscope image from the monitor, placed over the cart, never the face.
- 22.2–30.9 s: minimal HUD (corner brackets + "منظر داخلي للقولون") on the close endoscopic view.
- A progress line under the panel. Caption zone in the lower-middle safe area (y ≈ 1360–1580).
- 31.1–34.0 s: branded outro with the doctor's mark, name and specialty, "تابعونا للمزيد" and an **editable handle placeholder** (`@your.handle`, since no handle was provided).

**Branding.** The doctor's supplied identity is used throughout: **د. أبوبكر احمد سند · DR. ABOBAKR SANAD · أخصائي الباطنة والجهاز الهضمي والكبد والمناظير**, with their circular stomach-and-scope mark.
- **Header**, for the whole video: the mark, name and specialty in the top band, with the topic title below.
- **Watermark**, for the whole video: a small mark in the top-left corner of the footage, so it travels with the picture even if the clip is cropped or reposted.
- **Outro**: the mark, Arabic and English name, specialty, and the handle placeholder.
- **Thumbnail**: the mark and the name.

The mark is cut from the supplied PNG banner (`project/graphics/brand/brand_banner.png` → `scripts/cut_brand_mark.py`). That source is only about 240 px across, so it is shown at 240 px or smaller. A vector/high-resolution original would let it be used larger.

## Editing and re-rendering

Requirements: FFmpeg, Node 18+ with `playwright` (Chromium), Python 3 with `numpy scipy soundfile noisereduce opencv-contrib-python-headless` (and `faster-whisper` for `transcribe.py`).

```bash
cd medical-reel/project
mkdir -p source && cp /path/to/original.mp4 source/source.mp4   # only needed if build/ is missing
./build.sh                       # full rebuild -> ../output/
FORCE=1 ./build.sh               # also redo tracking/blur/frames/voice cleanup
node scripts/render.mjs --preview 1,9.6,17.5,25,33 --out build/preview   # quick stills
```

Common edits:
- **Texts, handle, doctor name/specialty, logo file**: `data/config.json` (`doctorName`, `doctorNameEn`, `specialty`, `handle`, `logo`).
- **Captions**: `data/transcript.txt`, then `./build.sh`.
- **Timing of zooms, split screen, magnifier, HUD, SFX**: `data/timeline.json`.
- **Look**: CSS variables at the top of `index.html` (`--navy`, `--teal`, `--cyan`, `--orange`).
- **Logo**: replace `graphics/brand/brand_mark.png` (or point `logo` in `config.json` at another file).
- **Music mood**: the chords, tempo and levels in `scripts/music.py`. Mix levels are in `scripts/mix.sh`.

Using this pipeline for a **different clip**: the monitor tracker's start quad and the blur keyframes in `scripts/anonymize.py` are specific to this recording. Re-check the blur on contact sheets before publishing. Also update `videoDur` and the cue times in `data/timeline.json`.
