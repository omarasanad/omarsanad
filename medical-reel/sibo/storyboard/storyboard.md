# SIBO reel: timestamped storyboard (v1)

The timings are **estimates** from the script length: about 95 s plus a 3 s branded outro. Once the doctor's recording exists, every cue is re-timed to his real words, using the same pipeline as earlier reels (caption timing anchored on speech).

## Format and look

- **Frame:** 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **Colours:** navy #0B1F3A, teal #14B8A6, cyan #3FE0D0, white; amber #FF9F45 only for "warning" accents (gas, toxins removed, etc.).
- **Type:** Cairo, as in the series.
- **Caption zone:** y ≈ 1400–1640.
- **Recurring elements:** the doctor's watermark in the top-left of every shot; the header used in earlier reels is dropped for this documentary style.

**Legend:**
- **DOC** – the doctor talking to camera (new recording, not yet supplied).
- **3D** – Blender render.
- **MG** – 2D motion graphics (HTML/canvas).
- **PiP** – the doctor in a round frame over B-roll.
