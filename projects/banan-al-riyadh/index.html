Banan Al Riyadh

Overview

Banan Al Riyadh is a premier residential development in Riyadh spanning over 10 million m², designed to accommodate 120,000 residents within a self-sustained, family-oriented community that blends Saudi heritage with modern living. The development includes educational and healthcare facilities, retail and leisure spaces, and expansive green areas — all designed to promote comfort, connectivity, and well-being. The project is being delivered for Talaat Moustafa Group, with Zuhair Fayez Partnership serving as design consultant, and includes the construction of 28 residential buildings.

Problem

Coordinating MEP and electrical systems across 28 residential buildings at this scale required continuously reconciling design changes between tender-issue and construction-issue drawings, while resolving conflicts between electrical, mechanical, and other building systems before they reached site.

Solution

Working from tender drawings, Bills of Quantities, and project specifications, I compared IFC (Issued for Construction) against IFT (Issued for Tender) drawing issues to track design changes, built coordinated BIM models across MEP disciplines, and produced clash detection reports. Missing, unclear, or conflicting design issues were resolved directly with the consultant through RFIs and technical coordination meetings, and electrical systems were coordinated with each other and with every other building system before shop drawings were submitted for construction.

My Role

  • Studied tender drawings, Bills of Quantities, and project specifications
  • Performed IFC vs. IFT drawing comparisons to track design changes
  • Created BIM models and clash detection reports
  • Coordinated MEP trades and studied all missing, unclear, conflicting, or other technical matters arising during construction, resolving them with the consultant via RFIs or technical meetings
  • Coordinated electrical systems with each other and with other building systems
  • Submitted shop drawings

Results

Contributing to the coordinated BIM delivery of Banan Al Riyadh for Talaat Moustafa Group — supporting MEP and electrical design coordination and construction-ready documentation across a 28-building residential community.

Lessons Learned

  • Comparing IFC and IFT drawing issues early catches design drift before it becomes a site problem.
  • Coordinating electrical systems against every other discipline reduces late-stage clashes on large, multi-building rollouts.
