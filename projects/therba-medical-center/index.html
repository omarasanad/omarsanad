Therba Medical Center

Overview

Located in the peaceful Tharba village, the Therba Medical Center is a state-of-the-art healthcare facility that blends functionality, cultural reverence, and architectural sophistication to serve the community's healthcare needs. The project was delivered for the Royal Commission for AlUla, with Khatib & Alami serving as design consultant.

Problem

Delivering a modern medical facility within a heritage-significant village required balancing functional healthcare infrastructure with the architectural character of the surrounding AlUla landscape, while ensuring MEP systems across every discipline were fully coordinated before construction began.

Solution

Working from tender drawings, Bills of Quantities, and project specifications, I built coordinated BIM models across MEP disciplines and produced clash detection reports to catch conflicts before they reached site. Missing, unclear, or conflicting design issues were resolved directly with the consultant through RFIs and technical coordination meetings, with shop and as-built drawings submitted as construction progressed.

My Role

  • Studied tender drawings, Bills of Quantities, and project specifications
  • Created BIM models and clash detection reports
  • Coordinated MEP trades and resolved missing, unclear, or conflicting technical matters with the consultant via RFIs and technical meetings
  • Submitted as-built and shop drawings

Results

Contributed to the coordinated BIM delivery of Therba Medical Center for the Royal Commission for AlUla — supporting design conflict resolution and construction-ready documentation for this heritage-context healthcare facility.

Lessons Learned

  • Raising RFIs early resolves design conflicts faster than waiting for issues to surface on site.
  • Heritage-sensitive project contexts add an extra layer of coordination between architectural intent and technical building systems.
