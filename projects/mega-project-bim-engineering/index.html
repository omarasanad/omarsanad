Mega-Project BIM Engineering

Overview

As a BIM Technical Office Engineer at BEC Arabia, I deliver BIM modeling, multidisciplinary MEP coordination, and technical submittals for large-scale developments in Saudi Arabia, including SINDALAH Island, Banan AlRiyadh, and AMAALA. A related project, the Therba Medical Center in Tharba, AlUla, has its own dedicated case study.

Problem

Mega-projects of this scale involve dozens of interdependent MEP trades working in parallel. Without rigorous coordination, clashes between electrical, mechanical, and structural systems surface late in construction — driving costly rework and schedule delays.

Solution

I build coordinated BIM models from LOD 300 through LOD 500, running clash detection across disciplines and resolving conflicts before they reach site. Shop and as-built drawings, along with technical submittals, are prepared and tracked through each project's document control workflow.

My Role

  • BIM Technical Office Engineer — model development and MEP trade coordination
  • Clash detection and resolution across multidisciplinary teams
  • Preparation of LOD 300–500 shop/as-built drawings and technical submittals

Results

Delivered coordinated, clash-resolved BIM models and complete LOD 300–500 documentation across four concurrent mega-projects, supporting on-schedule design submittals for multi-billion dollar developments.

Lessons Learned

  • Consistent LOD standards across trades early in a project prevent much larger coordination issues later.
  • Clear submittal tracking is as important as the modeling itself when managing multiple concurrent mega-projects.
