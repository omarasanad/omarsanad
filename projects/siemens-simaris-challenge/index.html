Siemens Global SIMARIS Challenge 2026 — 3rd Place Globally

Overview

The Siemens Global SIMARIS Challenge 2026 is an international competition where student teams design power distribution systems for real-world scenarios using Siemens' SIMARIS software suite. I served as Academic Advisor to a student team from Benha University, guiding them through a design covering a high-availability power supply system for an AI data center.

Problem

AI data centers demand extremely high power availability and resilience — even brief outages can be costly. The challenge required designing an electrical distribution architecture that maintains continuous power supply under redundancy and failure scenarios, while remaining efficient and standards-compliant.

Solution

The team developed a power system design using Siemens SIMARIS, applying redundancy planning and careful load/capacity calculations to meet high-availability requirements for critical data center infrastructure.

My Role

  • Academic Advisor — mentored the student team throughout the design and submission process
  • Reviewed technical design decisions and provided guidance on power system standards
  • Supported the team through each stage of the global competition

Gallery

Design diagrams and competition materials coming soon.

Results

The team placed 3rd globally among entries from around the world — a strong result reflecting the quality of the power system design and the team's engineering rigor.

Lessons Learned

  • Mentoring a competition team requires balancing technical guidance with letting students own their design decisions.
  • High-availability power design for data centers has distinct redundancy requirements compared to conventional distribution projects.
