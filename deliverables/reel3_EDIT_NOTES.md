## Source

The source is a vertical iPhone clip (1072×1904, 30 fps) with clean, neutral colour. I applied only a light contrast and saturation lift, plus light denoise. The audio was quiet (−32 LUFS), so I raised it by about 14.5 dB after noise reduction, EQ and gentle compression. I removed 5.6 s of pauses (49.7 s → 44.1 s).

The layouts are the same as reel 1: full-screen speaker with slow push-ins and a wide/close switch at every cut, a split screen with the graphic panel on top, and a full-screen graphic with the speaker in a corner circle.

## Medical accuracy note

The graphics only illustrate what the doctor says: a coating layer first, then fat and calcium increasing acid secretion, then rebound acidity. The curve is schematic, with no values, durations or percentages.

"الحموضة الارتدادية" is my Arabic rendering of the term "Rebound Hyperacidity", which he says in English.

"مركز النهضة الطبي · شبين القناطر" on the end card is not said in this video. I took it from the other two reels for consistent branding. Remove it if you prefer.
