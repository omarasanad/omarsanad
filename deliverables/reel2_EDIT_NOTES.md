# Reel 2 (patient care / Al-Nahda Medical Center): editing summary

**File:** `reel2_broll_1080x1920.mp4`. It is 1080×1920 (9:16), H.264 High at 60 fps, with AAC 256 kbps / 48 kHz audio. It runs 51.4 s: 48.9 s of speech plus a 2.5 s end card. Loudness is about −14.8 LUFS integrated, with peaks at −1 dBFS.

## Source and framing

The source is **horizontal** (832×464), so it was not cropped to fill a vertical frame. Filling 9:16 would have meant a 4× upscale of a small face. Instead the shot stays horizontal as a rounded "screen" on the brand-navy background:

- **Band:** the shot sits in the middle of the frame. Graphics appear above it and captions below.
- **Lower band:** the shot slides down. Graphics fill the top 860 px and captions sit between the graphics and the shot.
- **Full:** a full-screen graphic, with the speaker in a teal-ringed circle at the top right.

The framing alternates between wide and a 16% punch-in at every cut to hide jump cuts, with a slow drift inside each shot. I removed 10 s of pauses (58.9 s → 48.9 s) and partly corrected a warm colour cast.

## Sound

- **Narration:** the original voice. Noise was reduced, it was lightly EQ'd and compressed, then normalised.
- **Music:** an original ambient bed (same palette as reel 1, varied), ducked under the voice.
- **Sound effects:** soft whooshes on layout changes, pops and ticks on reveals, dings on check marks, and a thud for the target and the pin. All were synthesised for this video.

## Assets and licensing

These are the same sources as reel 1:

- Health Icons (MIT, by Resolve to Save Lives): person, stethoscope, magnifier, pills, heart, ear, hospital, doctors, X-ray, test tubes, general surgery, accident and emergency, stomach, liver, location.
- Medical Icons (MIT): internal medicine.
- Cairo font (SIL OFL 1.1).
- Your logo.

All other graphics, the music and the sound effects were made for this video. No stock footage or photographs were used, because the environment's network policy blocks the stock sites.
